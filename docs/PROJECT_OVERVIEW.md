# ASTROMIND — Project overview

**Project:** ASTROMIND  
**Team:** Orbit Smiths  
**Challenge:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — *Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars*  
**Design platform:** Figma  
**Primary artifact:** Interactive Figma prototype  
**Support artifacts:** Educational mission content, presentation script, GitHub Pages companion website

## The hook: imagine a forgotten rover

A machine travelled to another world, made extraordinary observations, then eventually fell silent. Its operating life ended — but its discoveries did not. The team's supplied 240-second pitch begins with this image and asks how those machines' stories might reach the next generation.

## The problem

The Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicles, Sojourner, and Opportunity contributed substantially to space exploration. Their technical histories, discoveries and media live across mission archives and scientific resources. School-age students may find it difficult to connect those fragmented resources into an engaging story.

## The proposed experience

ASTROMIND is a mission-based educational adventure. Students choose an astronaut, select the Moon or Mars, learn about a real machine and the science behind its mission, and answer knowledge-check questions. The main project is a Figma prototype; this repository's HTML website is a distinct communication and showcase surface.

## Educational goals

1. Introduce key mission hardware and distinguish its purpose from its scientific impact.
2. Help students understand why missions matter after a machine stops transmitting.
3. Encourage exploration of original NASA mission references.
4. Use quizzes to reinforce discoveries rather than simply memorizing names.

## Scope today

The repository includes supplied design screenshots and the presentation PDF, plus a static project website with a mission selector and a sample quiz. It does not contain a complete game runtime, an exported editable Figma source file, an API-backed science database, or verified student-learning outcomes.

## Potential future impact

The team plans more missions, interactive scientific investigations, real lunar/Martian maps, accessibility refinement and student user-testing. These are goals rather than completed features.
